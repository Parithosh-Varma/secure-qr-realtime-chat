How this mock works
  • QR-only — desktop shows only QR until scan. Mock uses local crypto.randomUUID + SHA-256 hex dm_· like Worker.
  • E2E — toggle shows enc:… payload; decryption is HKDF(token)→AES-GCM, same as desktop.js/mobile.js.
  • 2-person — third join blocked (“Room is full — only 2”).
  • Ephemeral — no localStorage, about:blank on refresh simulation, history never replayed.
Try it
  1. Click Simulate scan — peer joins, both see “E2E on”.
  2. Switch You / Peer and send — message appears on both sides, decrypted.
  3. Click Refresh in top bar or press F5 — mock blanks chat and regenerates QR (peer would close).
